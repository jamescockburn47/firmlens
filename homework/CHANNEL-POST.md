# FirmLens homework — channel post

Thanks for helping choose and build FirmLens in Saturday's session. The next step is to use our judgment and taste to improve it.

**Option 1 — try it and give feedback (15–20 minutes).**
Open https://firmlens.app and click around. What do you like? What is confusing, missing or awkward? Try a search or filter, open an entry, and compare its summary with the original source. Tell us what you would keep or change, and why. A short post with a link or screenshot is plenty. You do not need to code or create an account.

**Option 2 — propose a small change yourself (optional).**
Start with something you noticed while using the site. Create a free GitHub account, then let me know if you want to be added as a contributor and send me your username. I will invite you. The guide walks you through opening the project with your agent, making one change on your own branch, checking it locally and submitting a pull request for me to assess.

Please use the site before asking your agent to investigate. I want your observation and your reason for preferring the change—not a general AI-generated review of the code. A small, well-judged improvement is the aim. If your change concerns an AI summary, check the underlying source; fluent wording is not proof that it is right.

The accompanying pack contains the step-by-step guide, architecture diagram, AI instructions and a PR template. No hosting account or production credentials are needed.
