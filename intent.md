# FirmLens intent

Help a reader understand what law firms publicly say they are doing with AI, with a direct path from a summary to its evidence. Do not rank firms' real capabilities from publicity. Preserve differences between plans, pilots, deployment and independently demonstrated results.

Residents should be able to inspect this build, challenge its outputs and propose a small improvement whose benefit can be demonstrated. Browsing stays public and account-free. The owner controls updates. AI works through the owner's account; the app stores evidence, enforces permissions and retains history.

The migration changes hosting, database access and owner authentication. It must preserve the existing entries and complete source history and must not imply the new AI connection or schedule has passed before a real run proves it.
